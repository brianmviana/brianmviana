## About me

**Senior Software Engineer** with over 9 years of experience in **backend development**, working on high-complexity and large-scale projects for major financial institutions such as Itaú Unibanco (by Zup Innovation), Banco ITI (by Zup Innovation), and Banco Pan (by ioasys). Also involved in projects at large companies like LG (by GREat/UFC). Specialist in Java, Kotlin, Spring, AWS, and microservices architecture, with strong expertise in cloud environments and containerization using Docker and Kubernetes.

Currently at **Zup Innovation**, I serve as Tech Lead on the Gráfica IGA – Itaú Gestão de Ativos project, leading technical decisions, supporting architecture, and evolving the platform. I have also worked as a backend developer on strategic Banco ITI projects such as PIX, bank slip payments, payment hubs, and system migration to Itaú Unibanco.

I began my professional journey in 2014, driven by curiosity, passion for technology, and continuous learning. Over the years, I have participated in academic initiatives, applied research, and programming teaching. I am also an enthusiast of IoT and home automation, areas I continue to explore as a hobby and through personal projects.

I constantly strive to solve real problems with robust solutions, scale systems responsibly, and collaborate with high-performance teams. I value clear communication, continuous learning, and building solutions that create real impact.
